    85→# PART 2 — PRODUCT VISION
    86→
    87→WALLORA is not simply a wallpaper application.
    88→
    89→It is an AI-powered creative platform where users can discover visual content, communicate with AI, generate artwork, edit images, build projects, interact with creators, publish digital creations and monetize their work.
    90→
    91→The product should combine the best ideas behind:
    92→
    93→- Pinterest: visual discovery and inspiration.
    94→- ChatGPT: conversational AI assistance.
    95→- Canva: accessible creative tools.
    96→- Instagram: profiles and community.
    97→- Creative marketplaces: digital products and creator monetization.
    98→- AI image studios: image generation and editing.
    99→- Personalization applications: wallpapers, collections and digital aesthetics.
   100→
   101→These are product references, not instructions to copy their branding, interfaces or proprietary designs.
   102→
   103→## Core product promise
   104→
   105→A user should be able to enter WALLORA with an idea and leave with something they have created, discovered, organized, shared or purchased.
   106→
   107→Example:
   108→
   109→A user opens WALLORA and types:
   110→
   111→"I want to create a futuristic luxury bedroom design with black walls, golden lighting, a gaming setup and a large window overlooking Nairobi."
   112→
   113→WALLORA AI should understand the request, help refine it, select a suitable creative tool, generate a visual concept using an available model, display the result, and allow the user to save it inside a project.
   114→
   115→Another user might ask:
   116→
   117→"Give me five wallpapers that match my dark minimalist phone setup."
   118→
   119→WALLORA should understand the request and search the approved wallpaper library.
